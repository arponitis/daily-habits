Install it like an app

🍎 iPhone (Safari)
  1. Open the app and tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
🤖 Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the app and tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
